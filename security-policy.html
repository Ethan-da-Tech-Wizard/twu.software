Security policy

If you find a security problem with our website, we would like to hear about it. This is a small static site, and we handle reports personally.

How to report

Email ethan@twu.software with a description of the issue, the affected URL, and steps to reproduce it. Please do not include anyone else's personal data. Our contact details are also published in security.txt.

In scope

  • The site served at twu.software (the pages, scripts and assets we publish; response headers set by GitHub Pages are out of scope).

Out of scope

  • Social engineering or phishing of TWU LLC or anyone else.
  • Denial-of-service or load testing.
  • Third-party services, including GitHub Pages and our DNS or email providers. Please report those to the provider.
  • Physical attacks, and findings that require access to someone else's account or device.

Good-faith research and safe harbor

If you act in good faith, test only against twu.software, avoid accessing or changing data that is not yours, stop once you have shown the issue exists, and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it publicly, we will not pursue legal action against you for that research and we will treat it as authorized. This policy does not cover third parties, who may have their own rules.

What to expect

  • We aim to acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
  • We will not offer a bug bounty or other payment.
  • We make no promises about timelines or outcomes beyond that acknowledgement, though we will try to fix real issues sensibly and are happy to credit you if you wish.
